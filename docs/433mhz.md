# Optional 433 MHz receiver

The sensor path uses `rtl_433` at **433.920 MHz**, legacy FC0012 SDR serial `43300001`. `watchkeeper.py` normalizes Nexus-TH events; `rf_store.py` stores temperature, humidity, channel, identity and battery status. `jobs-sensors.jsonc` preserves 70 s reception/60 s handoff.

The [engineering log](project-log.md) records live sensor 132/channel 2 on 24 September: 13.0 °C, 94% humidity, battery OK. Subsequent measurements support Nexus-TH temperature/humidity reception; additional device families are not established by inspected evidence.

This workload is secondary/optional and **currently disabled/disconnected**. `rf-watchkeeper-sensors.service` and its health requirement are disabled. Dashboard history can remain visible without current reception.

Project records report legacy-receiver interference, including concern when it was not receiving. A physical mechanism, controlled comparison and verified remedy remain unestablished. Keep it disabled pending investigation. A future second required receiver needs integrated sample evidence and recovery ownership. See [hardware](hardware.md).
